Leon Sans is a geometric variable sans-serif typeface designed by Jongmin Kim. Originally created in 2019 as a code-driven drawing system to celebrate the birth of his son Leon, Version 2.0 brings the family into an installable OpenType variable font with a continuous weight axis from Thin (100) to Black (900).

Engineered on a 2000-UPM grid from eight UFO masters, Version 2.0 pairs 1:1 circular bowls with smoothly tapered curve-to-stem joins and harmonized figures across all nine weights. Its 470-glyph character set supports Google Fonts Latin Core and extended European Latin, superscripts, subscripts, fractions, currencies, arrows, and mathematical and scientific symbols, with OpenType kerning and mark positioning.

To contribute, visit github.com/cmiscm/leonsansfont. The JavaScript library is maintained at github.com/cmiscm/leonsans.
